Vishba squeezes the pummel of his sword, locks his eyes with Baji Prabhu and lunges at him serving a volley of firm blows. Baji deflects Vishba's sword with his shield, moving around the arena, not offering any chance to Vishba to pin him down like he did with his father. They step back, moving in unison. Vishba looking for opportune moment to surprise Baji with his dagger. Vishba charges at Baji again, serves a series of firm thrusts, all landing in the shield. Baji with his shield is a wall that Vishba is unable to pierce. Baji deflects the sword and locks his arm under his left arm, their faces close, staring at each other. perfect time for Vishba to draw his hidden dagger-- With his free left hand, Vishba draws his dagger and goes for Baji's ribs. Baji catches Vishba's hand midway. BAJI PRABHU (cont'd) You can't surprise me. Baji lets go of Vishba's sword arm and pushes Vishba back with the shield. Baji's turn -- He lunges forward with the shield and delivers a barrage of blows as if it were a hammer. Vishba tumbles backward, unable to hold off the brute force brought upon him by the shield. BAJI PRABHU (cont'd) Get up. Vishba regains his composure, locks his eyes on Baji and as he prepares his attack-- We hear SOUND OF HOOVES OF HORSES. Baji Prabhu is distracted. The boy seizes the opportunity and delivers a blow. Baji Prabhu brings up his shield, but a microsecond late. The sword slides on the shield and leaves a gash on his shoulder, letting a STREAM OF BLOOD. A GROUP OF RIDERS arrive through the gates. Moroji rushes to tend to Baji prabhu's wound.
MOROJI: Please accept my apologies. My son can sometimes be unruly. (looking angrily at Vishba) He shouldn't have struck when you were distracted.
BAJI PRABHU: Enemy doesn't wait till you are attentive, Moroji. You son has an eye for opponent's weaknesses. A distraction is a weakness on a battle field. Moroji holds a piece of cloth against the wound. BAJI PRABHU (cont'd) He has the strength you had twenty years ago. He can be a worthy addition to our force. Moroji looks at his son proudly. Vishba is elated. He touches Baji Prabhu's feet.

BAJI PRABHU: Dada, what is the reason for your sudden visit?

BAJI PRABHU: By whom?
PHULAJI: Shivaji. His men have already raided lands around the fort. It is only a matter of time before he attacks the fort.
BAJI PRABHU: Ready our men. Let's go inside while my men get ready.
